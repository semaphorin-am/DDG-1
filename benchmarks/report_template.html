Geometry and seams

Regular and perturbed sphere meshes

Same connectivity; vertices are moved tangentially by a fraction of the local edge length and put back on the sphere. Triangles are shaded by their smallest angle. Drag to rotate; both views turn together.

Flat torus: the periodic rectangle and the wraparound route

The torus is the rectangle 1.5 × 1 with left/right and bottom/top edges identified (matching edge marks). Neighbouring copies are drawn faintly. Click or drag anywhere to move the target.

Exact vs computed: geodesic distance

Left and middle share one colour scale, drawn in 16 equal distance bands; the right panel is computed minus exact on a symmetric scale. The exact field is recomputed in this page from the vertex positions; the computed field comes from the benchmark output.

Exact vs computed: heat diffusion

A Gaussian bump placed next to the corner of the rectangle, so heat must leave through both seams and re-enter on the far sides. The exact solution is the periodised Gaussian with variance s₀² + 2t.

Fourier-mode decay

Each analytic mode is evolved to a fixed time and the surviving amplitude compared with exp(−λT). Tolerance: relative rate error ≤ λh². Mode (0,0) is the constant and must not change at all.
