## Precalculus Connection
Elias is preparing for a Precalculus course (syllabus includes trigonometry, vectors, matrices, 3D geometry, spherical coordinates, etc.). When explaining math concepts, **connect them to the corresponding Precalculus topics:**
- **Lesson 5-6:** Law of Cosines/Sines → computing angles and areas in Stage 8
- **Lesson 8:** Spherical Coordinates → the unit sphere and 3D coordinates (Stage 3)
- **Lesson 14-15:** 3D Vectors & Matrices → vertices and incidence matrices (Stages 2-7)
- **Lesson 19-22:** 3D Matrices, Cross Products → Laplacian, normals, full pipeline (Stages 6-10)

## Key Insights
- **Stage transitions are steep:** Preview new Python concepts before challenges.
- **Productive struggle works:** Elias learns deeply by struggling and iterating. Don't rush to solutions.
- **Wants understanding:** Walks through code line-by-line after completion.
- **Pays attention to detail:** Catches inconsistencies and asks clarifying questions.

## How to Help
- Guide with questions, not answers
- Explain the "why" behind design choices and connect to Precalculus when relevant
- Preview new concepts when moving to a new stage
- Celebrate struggle as part of learning
- Be concise but complete in explanations
